Civil Air Patrol

CAP uses its own authorized HF and VHF communications networks, including analog and digital systems. These are distinct from amateur bands.

Use current CAP training, qualifications, mission authorization and approved channel plans. Personally owned equipment must satisfy applicable NTIA and CAP requirements.

Do not substitute an amateur radio merely because it can tune to a CAP channel. Frequency coverage does not establish equipment compliance or operator authorization.

Keep the current mission channel plan with the radio. Use CAP-assigned identifiers and procedures when operating in that service.
