Review before the next outing

This edition is dated 2026-10-31. Recheck rules, band privileges, software frequency lists and program instructions before relying on an older printed copy.

Verify local channels, tones, nets, contacts and paths before every deployment. Worksheets intentionally leave these operational assignments for local confirmation.

The source pages identify the references used. Source numbers on topic pages point to those entries. Original procedures and worksheets supplement those references.

Maintain your own change log and replace revised leaves in the comb. Keep one complete electronic copy available offline.
