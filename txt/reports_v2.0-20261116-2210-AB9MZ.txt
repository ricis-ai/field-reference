Signal reporting

Signal reports

Voice usually uses readability and strength, such as five seven. CW adds tone, such as five seven nine. Give an honest report.

Readability Description
1 Unreadable
2 Occasional words understood
3 Readable with difficulty
4 Readable with little difficulty
5 Perfectly readable

On FM, clear audio or noisy but readable is often more useful than a meter number. FT8 reports signal-to-noise ratio in dB, not RST.

Strength and tone

Strength Description
1 Barely perceptible
2 Very weak
3 Weak
4 Fair
5 Fairly good
6 Good
7 Moderately strong
8 Strong
9 Extremely strong

CW tone 9 is a pure note. Lower tone numbers indicate increasing roughness or AC character. S meters are not uniformly calibrated.

CW tone scale

Tone is the third digit of a CW RST report. These traditional descriptions are rarely needed with a clean modern transmitter.

Tone Description
1 Extremely rough AC note
2 Very rough, unmusical
3 Rough, low musical quality
4 Moderately rough
5 Musical with rough modulation
6 Modulated musical note
7 Near pure, trace of ripple
8 Near pure, slight ripple
9 Pure musical note

Report a real problem when present; do not assign 9 automatically.
